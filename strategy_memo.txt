SMART DELIVERY ETA STRATEGY MEMO

Project Overview:
This project uses graph-based network intelligence to optimize delivery ETA prediction and logistics operations.

Key Features Implemented:
- Real-time traffic delay simulation
- Bottleneck hub detection
- AI route optimization
- SLA breach forecasting
- Revenue-at-risk estimation
- Multiple rider tracking
- Real-time delivery dashboard
- FTL vs Carting decision AI
- Graph AI vs Baseline ML comparison

Business Impact:
The system improves delivery prediction accuracy, reduces SLA breaches, and helps optimize logistics operations.

Key Bottlenecks Identified:
1. Hub A congestion
2. Delayed warehouse dispatch
3. Traffic-heavy delivery corridors

Recommendations:
- Upgrade hub processing capacity
- Add alternate delivery corridors
- Improve rider allocation strategy
- Optimize dispatch timing

Conclusion:
Graph-based intelligence significantly improves logistics decision-making and delivery ETA prediction compared to traditional methods.
